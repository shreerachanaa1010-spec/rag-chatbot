# US Health Insurance Benefits Summary
**Region:** United States
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Document ID:** HR-POL-US-021

## Plan Options
- PPO Gold Plan: $150/month employee premium, $500 individual deductible.
- PPO Silver Plan: $90/month employee premium, $1,500 individual deductible.
- HDHP + HSA Plan: $40/month employee premium, $3,200 individual deductible,
  Company contributes $1,000/year to employee HSA.

## Eligibility
Full-time employees (30+ hours/week) are eligible from day 1 of employment.
Part-time employees are not eligible for Company-subsidized health plans.

## Dependent Coverage
Spouses, domestic partners, and dependent children up to age 26 may be
added during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event
(marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage).

## Open Enrollment
Held annually in November for coverage effective January 1. Changes outside
open enrollment require a qualifying life event and must be reported to
HR within 30 days of the event.
