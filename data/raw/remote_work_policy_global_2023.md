# Global Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
**Region:** Global (all offices)
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2023
**Document ID:** HR-POL-GLOBAL-030

## Work Arrangements
- **Hybrid** (default): Minimum 3 days/week in the assigned office.
- **Full Remote**: Requires Director-level approval and is only available
  for roles explicitly designated "remote-eligible" by the role's job code.
- **Full-Time Office**: Required for roles that handle physical records,
  regulated healthcare data on-site, or lab/clinical work.

## Equipment
Company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home-office stipend
for approved full-remote employees. Hybrid employees are not eligible for
the home-office stipend.

## Cross-Border Remote Work
Employees may not work remotely from a country other than their country of
employment for more than 20 consecutive business days without prior approval
from HR and Legal, due to tax and labor law implications.

## Review Cadence
Manager and employee review the work arrangement every 6 months.
