# US Parental Leave Policy - 2023 Amendment
**Region:** United States
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2023
**Document ID:** HR-POL-US-014 (v2.0) - SUPERSEDES v1.0

## Summary of Changes
Effective March 1, 2023, the Company has expanded parental leave benefits
for all US-based full-time employees. This amendment supersedes and replaces
the parental leave terms described in HR-POL-US-014 (v1.0, dated Jan 1, 2021)
in their entirety.

## Eligibility
Employees must have completed at least 60 days of continuous employment
(reduced from 90 days) prior to the qualifying event.

## Leave Entitlement
- Birthing parent: 12 weeks of fully paid leave (increased from 8 weeks).
- Non-birthing parent / secondary caregiver: 8 weeks of fully paid leave
  (increased from 4 weeks).
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event (increased
  from 6 months).

## Pay During Leave
100% of base salary, unchanged from prior policy. Leave does not count
against standard PTO balance.

## How to Apply
Same process as before: submit Form HR-014 to your manager and to
hr-leaves@company.com at least 30 days before the anticipated start date.
