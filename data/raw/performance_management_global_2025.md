# Global Performance Management Policy
**Region:** Global (all offices)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Document ID:** HR-POL-GLOBAL-041

## Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes a consistent performance-management process for regular employees worldwide. Local law, works council requirements, and collective bargaining agreements apply where they impose additional requirements.

## Goal Setting
Managers and employees must agree on role goals within 30 days of the start of each performance year or within 30 days of a transfer or promotion. Goals should identify measurable outcomes, role expectations, development priorities, and any dependencies outside the employee's control.

## Check-ins
Managers must hold a documented one-to-one check-in at least once each quarter. Check-ins should cover progress, priorities, support needed, feedback, wellbeing, and changes to goals. Employees may request an additional check-in at any time.

## Annual Review
The annual review cycle closes on December 31. Managers provide a written assessment using the Company's performance factors: results, collaboration, customer impact, judgment, and role-specific capability. Employees may submit written comments within 10 business days of receiving the review.

A performance rating must be supported by documented examples from the review period. Protected characteristics, approved leave, disability accommodations, complaints made in good faith, and legally protected activity must not influence a rating.

## Performance Improvement Plan
A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be used when performance is materially below role expectations after coaching and feedback. A PIP normally lasts 60 to 90 days and must state the performance gaps, measurable expectations, support offered, check-in dates, and possible outcomes. HR must review a PIP before it is issued.

## Calibration and Appeals
People leaders and HR may calibrate ratings to promote consistency across teams. Employees who believe a review contains a factual error or prohibited bias should first discuss it with the manager and may contact HR within 30 calendar days.
