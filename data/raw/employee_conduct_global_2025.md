# Global Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy
**Region:** Global (all offices)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Document ID:** HR-POL-GLOBAL-042

## Standards of Conduct
Employees must act lawfully, honestly, respectfully, and in the Company's legitimate interests. The standards apply at Company locations, during business travel, at work events, in digital collaboration spaces, and when representing the Company online.

Prohibited conduct includes harassment, discrimination, bullying, retaliation, threats, violence, conflicts of interest that are not disclosed, misuse of confidential information, falsification of records, and intentional misuse of Company systems or assets.

## Reporting Concerns
Employees may report a concern to their manager, Human Resources, Legal, the Ethics Hotline, or any other manager they trust. Reports may be made verbally or in writing. Employees do not need to report to the person involved in the concern.

Ethics Hotline: ethics@company.com
Confidential reporting portal: ethics.company.com/report

## Investigation Process
The Company will acknowledge reports when possible, assess immediate safety or legal risks, and assign an impartial investigator. Participants must preserve relevant records and cooperate honestly. Investigations are conducted as confidentially as practical, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

The investigator documents relevant evidence and provides findings to HR and Legal. Corrective action may include coaching, training, written warning, reassignment, loss of access, or termination, depending on the facts and applicable law.

## Non-Retaliation
Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith, participates in an investigation, requests an accommodation, or exercises a legal right is prohibited. Suspected retaliation should be reported promptly through any channel listed above.

## Employee Responsibilities
Employees must complete assigned conduct and anti-harassment training, protect investigation confidentiality, and report urgent safety concerns immediately to local emergency services or Security.
