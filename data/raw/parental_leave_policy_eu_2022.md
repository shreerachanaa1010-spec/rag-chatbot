# EU Parental Leave Policy
**Region:** European Union (Germany, France, Netherlands offices)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2022
**Document ID:** HR-POL-EU-014

## Overview
This policy applies to all full-time employees based in EU member-state
offices, in addition to statutory parental leave entitlements provided by
local law, whichever is more generous to the employee.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible from their first day of employment,
consistent with EU Directive 2019/1158 on work-life balance for parents.

## Leave Entitlement
- Birthing parent: 16 weeks fully paid leave, plus any additional statutory
  maternity leave required by local law.
- Non-birthing parent / secondary caregiver: 10 weeks fully paid leave.
- Leave may be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event, or as
  otherwise permitted by local statutory law.

## Pay During Leave
100% of base salary, coordinated with statutory maternity/parental pay
schemes to avoid double payment.

## How to Apply
Submit local country HR leave form (see country-specific intranet page)
at least 60 days before the anticipated start date, in accordance with
local statutory notice requirements.
