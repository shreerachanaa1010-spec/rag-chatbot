# US Compensation and Rewards Policy
**Region:** United States
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Document ID:** HR-POL-US-040

## Scope
This policy applies to regular full-time and part-time US employees. It covers base pay, annual merit reviews, incentive eligibility, promotions, and compensation changes. Collective bargaining agreements and local law control where they provide more favorable terms.

## Pay Philosophy
The Company sets pay using market data, internal role levels, skills, sustained performance, and geographic labor-market considerations. Compensation decisions must be consistent, explainable, and free from discrimination or retaliation.

## Salary Bands
Each job level has a published minimum, midpoint, and maximum salary range. The employee's range and current compa-ratio are available through the HR portal. Hiring managers may not promise compensation outside the approved range without Compensation Team approval.

## Annual Merit Review
Annual merit reviews are normally effective April 1. Employees must be active and in good standing on the review date. Merit increases are based on performance, position in the salary range, market movement, and available budget. A merit review does not guarantee an increase.

Managers must submit written recommendations to HR by February 15. Employees returning from approved protected leave must be evaluated on the same performance period and may not be penalized because of the leave.

## Promotions and Market Adjustments
A promotion requires a documented increase in job scope and approval from the manager, department leader, and Compensation Team. Promotion increases normally take effect at the start of the next payroll period after approval. Market adjustments may be made when reliable market data shows a material gap; they are separate from performance increases.

## Bonuses and Incentives
Eligible employees may participate in an annual incentive plan. Target percentages are determined by job level and plan document. Actual payment depends on Company results, individual performance, and active-employment requirements in the applicable plan. Incentive payments are discretionary unless a written plan states otherwise.

## Payroll and Pay Questions
Employees should report a suspected payroll error through the HR portal within 30 days of the pay date. Payroll investigates and corrects confirmed errors in the next practicable payroll cycle. Employees should not send bank details or tax documents through ordinary email.
