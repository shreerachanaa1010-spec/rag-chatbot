# US Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
**Region:** United States
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Document ID:** HR-POL-US-007

## Overview
Applies to all full-time, non-union US employees.

## Accrual
- Years 0-2 of service: 15 PTO days/year, accrued at 1.25 days/month.
- Years 3-5 of service: 20 PTO days/year, accrued at 1.67 days/month.
- Years 6+ of service: 25 PTO days/year, accrued at 2.08 days/month.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31, unless local state
law requires otherwise (e.g., California does not permit "use-it-or-lose-it"
policies; unused PTO carries over in full for CA-based employees).

## Requesting PTO
Submit requests through the WorkdayHR portal at least 5 business days in
advance for planned absences. Manager approval is required.

## Payout on Termination
Unused, accrued PTO is paid out at the employee's final base rate upon
termination, in accordance with state law.
