# Global Career Development and Internal Mobility Policy
**Region:** Global (all offices)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Document ID:** HR-POL-GLOBAL-043

## Purpose and Eligibility
The Company supports employees in building skills, exploring career paths, and applying for internal roles. Regular employees may participate in development programs from their first day, subject to role requirements, business priorities, and local law.

## Individual Development Plans
Employees and managers should review an Individual Development Plan at least twice each year. The plan may include skill goals, mentoring, project assignments, certifications, job shadowing, and formal learning. Development goals do not change the employee's current job level or guarantee promotion.

## Learning Budget
Eligible full-time employees may receive up to $1,500 per calendar year for approved external courses, certifications, or conferences. Part-time eligibility is prorated by scheduled hours. Requests must be submitted through the Learning Portal before registration and must include the business purpose, provider, cost, and expected completion date.

The manager and People Operations must approve the request before the employee incurs an expense. Reimbursement requires proof of payment and completion within 30 days after the learning activity.

## Mentoring and Career Conversations
Employees may request a mentor through the internal mentoring program. Managers should hold a career conversation at least twice per year and discuss skills needed for possible next roles. Managers must not retaliate against employees who explore internal opportunities.

## Internal Job Applications
Employees may apply for posted internal roles after completing six months in their current role, unless HR approves an exception for restructuring, relocation, or business-critical circumstances. Employees must meet the qualifications listed in the posting. Current managers are informed after the candidate enters the interview stage, unless local law or HR guidance requires another process.

Selection decisions are based on qualifications, documented performance, business needs, and fair hiring practices. Applying for an internal role does not guarantee an interview, transfer, or promotion.
