# US Parental Leave Policy
**Region:** United States
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2021
**Document ID:** HR-POL-US-014 (v1.0)

## Overview
This policy applies to all full-time employees based in the United States.

## Eligibility
Employees must have completed at least 90 days of continuous employment prior
to the expected date of birth, adoption, or foster placement.

## Leave Entitlement
- Birthing parent: 8 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Non-birthing parent / secondary caregiver: 4 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Leave must be taken within 6 months of the qualifying event.

## Pay During Leave
100% of base salary is paid during the leave period through the Company's
short-term disability and parental leave benefit program. Leave does not
count against the employee's standard PTO balance.

## How to Apply
Submit Form HR-014 to your manager and to hr-leaves@company.com at least
30 days before the anticipated start date (or as soon as practicable for
unplanned events such as premature birth).
